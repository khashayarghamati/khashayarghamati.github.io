About Me

I’m Khashayar -- an AI engineer working on systems that keep learning after they’re deployed.

As Principal AI Engineer at Inframatic Engineering, I lead adaptive AI for compliance automation in critical infrastructure -- knowledge-graph retrieval, multi-agent architectures, and computer vision, built to run in production rather than in a notebook. Behind that sits a decade of software engineering and a habit of making research-grade ideas reliable enough to ship.

Alongside that, I distilled a 72B language model into a 1.5B agent released on Hugging Face, and I build transformers from scratch to understand the models I optimise.

That practice is grounded in published work. I defended my doctoral thesis at the University of Hertfordshire in June 2026 -- NOVA, a framework for novelty detection and continual learning in human–robot interaction, validated on the ARI humanoid -- and I still supervise BSc and MSc students there. My work appears in IEEE TCDS, RO-MAN, ICSR and Sensors; I review for IEEE TCDS and co-organised SS03 at ICSR 2026.

If you’d like to get in touch about collaboration, speaking, or adaptive AI in real-world systems -- say hello.

Research & Engineering Projects

HAR-Agent -- distilled multimodal LLM agent
2025--2026
LLM-centred activity-recognition agent over a vision/audio/text harness. 17 configurations compared from 1.5B to 72B parameters (instruction tuning vs. supervised fine-tuning); 72B teacher distilled to a 1.5B student at 48× compression, running in under 1 GB of GPU memory; 89.2% on a five-language audio benchmark. Model on Hugging Face ↗
ARTEMIS -- short-term memory for vision-language agents
2025--2026
Short-term-memory mechanism aggregating LLaVA-Next frame descriptions before Qwen2.5 reasoning; cuts semantic redundancy by 72.5% across 29 activities at ~2 FPS entirely on local hardware. Project page ↗
Transformers from scratch (PyTorch)
2025--2026
GPT-2-class decoder-only LLM (BPE tokenisation, causal multi-head attention, KV-cache inference, pre-training, instruction fine-tuning with loss masking); MusicGen-style text-to-music transformer (EnCodec RVQ tokens, delay pattern, cross-attention over frozen T5, classifier-free guidance) trained with hand-written GPipe-style pipeline parallelism on 2× RTX A6000 and released as an open model; LLaVA-style VLM (frozen ViT → MLP projector → LLM). Each built in verified stages to understand the architectures from first principles. Music-LLM project page ↗ · Read the story → · Model on Hugging Face ↗
